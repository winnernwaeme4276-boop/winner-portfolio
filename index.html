A little about me

Building today.
Thinking bigger tomorrow.

I'm Winner Nwaeme, a Computer Science student at Anchor University, Lagos, Nigeria, and an aspiring frontend web developer.

I'm passionate about technology, problem-solving and creating digital experiences that are both useful and visually appealing.

My development journey focuses on HTML, CSS, JavaScript and responsive web design. I use AI-assisted development tools to support my workflow while continuing to strengthen my programming knowledge through practical projects.

Beyond web development, I have a growing background in computer networking. I've earned the MikroTik Certified Network Associate (MTCNA) certification and a Certificate of Excellence in MikroTik Computer Networking from Anchor University.

My experience in sales and customer support has also helped me understand the importance of communication, customer needs and delivering practical solutions.

I'm currently building my portfolio through independent projects while developing the skills needed to work with businesses and individuals.

My long-term ambition is to establish Unlimited Industry, a group of businesses focused on technology, financial services, commerce and infrastructure.

I believe every major achievement starts with learning, consistency and taking the first step.
